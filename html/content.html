> content

jell.c publishes two layers of content: public and paid. The public layer will always remain free. The paid layer offers a deeper dive into the material.

Free

No paywall. No drip. We posted content and that will remain publicly available.

  • - Videos — advanced programming concepts, launching soon
  • - Source code — repositories and gists to accompany the videos

Paid

Same subjects, more surface area. Built for people who learn by reading the code and breaking it on purpose. We will offer additional resources to accompany the videos.

  • - Full source code — the examples from the videos, complete and buildable
  • - Problem sets — exercises with solutions, not quizzes
  • - Extended cuts — dive deeper into the material
  • - Office hours — live coding sessions, with Q&A
